Run AI models entirely on your phone.

No internet. No cloud. No data leaves your device.

TinyModels downloads open large-language models (in .litertlm format) and runs them locally with on-device inference. Your chats, photos, and files stay on your phone.

Star on GitHub Read the Privacy Policy

How your data is handled

  • Chats stay on your device. Conversations are stored in the app's private on-device database only.
  • No account, no analytics. The app ships with no login and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Network is used only when you ask. Model downloads from Hugging Face, optional web search, and downloadable fonts are the only connections the app makes — see the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Open source. The code is public at github.com/vengateshk18/TinyModels, so these claims are verifiable.
