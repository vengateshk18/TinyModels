# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in TinyModels a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

**Welcome:** empathy, constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the
community, gracefully accepting other viewpoints.

**Unacceptable:** sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or
political attacks, harassment (public or private), publishing others' private
information without permission, or any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
and issues that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for harmful behavior.

Reports of violations: **vengatesh00014@gamil.com**. All complaints are handled
confidentially and taken seriously.
